## Decisions & Learnings
- Absolute-ness lives in exactly one place (`CircuitPrimitives.is_absolute`);
  runtime transforms, replay restore, analyzer sampling, and envelopes all
  branch on it, so nonzero peg bases can never double-translate a path.
- The bounds exemption is deliberately narrow: assignment to a closed,
  compiled, smooth path circuit. Opening the path or breaking a join disables
  the exemption, so malformed authoring cannot smuggle pegs past the filter.
- `Path:at_distance` was ~5 ms per call (48 bisections, each re-integrating
  the cubic). `luajit tools/check_level.lua` took 2m54s and the 87-peg level
  would have been unplayable. The arc-length table is ~0.32 µs/eval
  (≈15 000×) with a measured maximum deviation of 3.2e-4 px and 7.3e-7
  tangent error over the level loop; endpoint evaluation stays exact.
- Same-path pairs are swept in common-advance space so long pause duty cycles
  cannot hide the moving phase; the reported `best_t` is converted back
  through the move/pause schedule (`elapsed_for_advance`), keeping elapsed-time
  semantics.
- Envelopes use conservative segment hulls instead of sampling, so a loop's
  extremes cannot fall between samples.
- Replay keeps the schema-2 model; continuation was verified step-for-step
  against uninterrupted simulation across the seam and pause transitions.
