# Dev Log - 2026-09-12

## Progress Summary

## Changes Made
- Added `src/systems/slide_inversion.lua`: per-ball zero-gap contact tracking,
  speed-independent apex detection from the contact normal's signed tangential
  speed (`tang = n.x·v.y − n.y·v.x`), and lowest-hit-peg selection by impact-y
  with tie tolerance.
- `Engine` (`src/engine/core.lua`): instantiate/reset the processor

## Decisions & Learnings
- The first detector used an absolute speed threshold (150 px/s) to record a
  direction. A real valley swing (slide level arc) peaks at ~90 px/s, so the
  apex was never recognized and the ball oscillated for many periods. The fix
  keys off the contact surface normal instead: the tangential-velocity sign
  flips exactly at the apex at any speed.
- Removal is board-only: a removed peg stays in `turnPegsHit`, so its points
  are still awarded; only the obstacle disappears.
- A genuine `did_bounce` resyncs the direction so a rebound is not mistaken
  for a pendulum apex.
