# Dev Log - 2026-10-01

## Progress Summary
- Fixed the squiggly-circle border tunneling bug from
  `temp/tunneling_investigation.md`: a ball touching an annular border's inner
  wall was pushed outward through the 20 px band by
  `2 * ball_radius + thickness` (~32 px) in a single fixed step.
- The annular discrete overlap check and the end-of-frame `depenetrate`
  fallback are now side-aware: they push through the surface the ball center
  actually overlaps, and exit the solid band through the nearer surface.
- Added `tests/test_annular_depenetration.lua` with three focused regression
  tests. The two overlap tests fail on the pre-fix code.

## Changes Made
- `src/systems/physics.lua` (`checkAnnulars`, discrete overlap check): the
  catch zone spans both sides of the wall, so it now classifies the ball
  center. Center in the hole (`dist < r_in`) pushes inward with
  `dist - (r_in - ball.radius)` and the inward normal. Center in the band
  (`r_in <= dist < r_out`) or outside exits through the nearer surface.
  Inner exits require `r_in > ball.radius`; the outer catch bound is unchanged.
- `src/systems/physics.lua` (`depenetrate`, annular block): the band case now
  compares inner and outer clearance and exits through the nearer surface
  instead of always pushing outward. The hole case keeps its inward push and
  gains the same `r_in > ball.radius` guard.
- `tests/test_annular_depenetration.lua` (new): inner-wall overlap on a
  large-radius, tiny-span sector stays inside the hole; a ball centered in the
  band exits through the nearer surface (both directions); a ball with full
  clearance is untouched by a physics step.
- `temp/tunneling_investigation.md`: recorded the applied fix, corrected the
  regression-failure note, and added verification results.

## Decisions & Learnings
- The old catch-zone predicate covered both sides of the wall but always used
  the outward normal and `(ball.radius + r_out) - dist` as penetration. For a
  ball resting on the inner wall of a thin border that formula reports
  `2 * ball.radius + thickness`, so the solver teleported the ball to the far
  side while barely changing velocity. Side-awareness is the fix; no solver
  change was needed.
- The band exit rule matches `ImpactGeometry.surface_separation`, which already
  treats `d < r_in` and the band with nearest-surface semantics. The physics
  resolution now agrees with the instrumentation geometry.
- Inner exit requires `r_in > ball.radius`. When the hole is smaller than the
  ball, the inward overlap formula is meaningless (it can overshoot the arc
  center), so the code falls back to the outer surface instead.
- The cap epsilon (`0.001` rad, ~8.7 px at the failing arc radius) was left
  alone as the investigation recommended. With the side fix the over-epsilon
  case is a sub-pixel inward nudge: the worst annular overlap in the fixed
  impact logs is 0.30 px.
- The replay traces also confirmed the fix is local: large jumps that remain
  are fall-out/respawn transitions where the ball array shifts, not wall
  tunneling.
