## Decisions & Learnings
- The re-capture needed no investigation: both `--check` modes pass and
  Julia replays every new record and trace byte-for-byte, as the 10ee3a0
  port verification predicted. `shot_bench.jl --fixture b1_center --level b1`
  reproduces the Lua expected record and trace byte-for-byte.
- Pure unit tests that build their own geometry keep the pinned classic
  config; they are the remaining `RULES_CLASSIC` coverage.
- Allocation under the 10ee3a0 rules: every one of the 12 real-level rows
  steps with 0 bytes (total and max single step), with ordinary and prepared
  checkpoints, and so does `m1_multi3`. Ordinary resets allocate 24,544 to
  30,784 bytes per candidate. Prepared resets allocate 1,168 bytes per
  candidate on every level. That figure predates the 10ee3a0 port and is
  recorded, not fixed. Records: `results/allocation-gate/2026-11-12-*.json`.
