# Dev Log - 2026-10-28

## Progress Summary

Replaced static strided campaign assignments with a shared FIFO queue of match
IDs. Persistent worker tasks take another match as soon as they finish; the
number of consumers remains bounded by the requested workers and match count.

## Changes Made

The queue is populated and closed before consumers start, avoiding a blocked
producer if a match throws. Each match still owns its live/candidate engines,
scratch and RNGs; seeds and output ordering depend on match ID. Timing records
now include the actual logical worker index, and the benchmark exports it to
`matches.csv` instead of inferring the former static assignment. Legacy timing
records retain the old fallback. Candidate evaluation remains serial per match.

## Verification

The local Julia suite passed 14,138/14,138 checks with three threads. Campaign
coverage compares five two-turn matches against sequential histories at one,
two and three consumers, with and without phase instrumentation, and checks
match order, worker IDs and requested concurrency exceeding the match count.
The benchmark harness suite passed 246/246 checks. Frozen-reference verification
passed before and after local verification.
