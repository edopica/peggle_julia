# Dev Log - 2026-10-28

## Progress Summary

Replaced static strided campaign assignments with a shared FIFO queue of match
IDs. Persistent worker tasks take another match as soon as they finish; the
number of consumers remains bounded by the requested workers and match count.

## Changes Made

The queue is populated and closed before consumers start, avoiding a blocked
producer if a match throws. Each match still owns its live/candidate engines,
scratch and RNGs; seeds and output ordering depend on match ID. Timing records
now include the actual logical worker index, and the benchmark exports it to
`matches.csv` instead of inferring the former static assignment. Legacy timing
records retain the old fallback. Candidate evaluation remains serial per match.

## Prepared checkpoints and compact geometry (§17.1 experiment 4)

Candidate evaluation now prepares an owned checkpoint once per decision,
decodes the RNG once, and restores peg/initial-ball objects from worker-owned
pools. Prepared indices reconstruct the current ordered shape lists, including
after removal and repeated resets. External checkpoint serialization and hashes
are unchanged. Inline numerical records feed the broad-phase scans; scoring,
metadata, precise tests and ordered contact reduction retain live peg references.

The first implementation refreshed compact geometry every frame and regressed
campaign time. The retained campaign path explicitly promises static geometry:
its artifacts have no circuits and its callbacks do not change peg transforms
or velocities. General prepared-engine callers retain refresh by default. Both
experiments remain in the evidence archive; `compact=false` isolates pooled reset.

On Deathstar, three interleaved repetitions per variant used ten ribbon 1v1
matches, eight workers/threads, seed 20261024, all 1,801 aims, full warmup,
Julia 1.12.6 and `system_libm`. Median elapsed time was **32.292 seconds** for
the combined implementation versus **33.438** for baseline: **−3.43%** time,
**+3.55%** throughput, and **−90.84%** allocated bytes. All three paired
combined/baseline ratios were positive. Comparisons with prepared reset alone
changed direction; no independent geometry throughput gain is claimed. All
variants slowed in the final repetition, and this fixed batch does not establish
sustained-load or SMT/NUMA results. Allocation reduction is not an RSS reduction.

The final local suite passed **80,453/80,453** checks. Deathstar's final focused
