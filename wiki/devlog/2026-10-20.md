## Decisions & Learnings

- **Port behavior, not intent.** Lua's `is_extra and nil or circ` always
  returns `circ`, because `nil` is falsy. The Julia port had "corrected" the
  apparent intent, which silently changed contact-event metadata. Frozen
  reference behavior wins even when it looks like a bug. The minimal check is
  `luajit -e 'local c={x=480}; print((true and nil or c).x)'`, which prints
  480.
- **Event metadata is part of parity.** The divider divergence never changed
  physics, score, or summaries, yet it broke trace bytes. Trace-level
  comparison is what caught it, so summary agreement alone is not sufficient
  evidence.
- **The Lua side is the pinned tree, deliberately.** The dev tree currently
  lacks `src/levels/circle_level.lua` (needed by `circles_small`) and its
  config uses `ball_ball_restitution = 0.7` versus the pinned/interchange
  `0.8`. All 7 workloads are pinned levels, and the 5 level sources are
  byte-identical between the pinned tree and the dev tree's HEAD; the dev
  working tree is what differs. `scripts/verify_reference.sh` passes 133/133.
  The M4 evidence must be read as pinned-vs-Julia, not dev-tree-vs-Julia.
- **Faithful scenario port (option a).** All 11 boundary scenarios are ported
  literally rather than reduced to a subset; no gap remains and no
  `scenario-gaps.json` was written.
- **No tolerance, no edits to expected values.** Every comparison is exact
  `==`; Int/Float and `-0`/`0` JSON representations are recorded but are not
  numeric differences.
