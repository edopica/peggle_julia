### Decisions & Learnings
- Solo catches return a ball and add no bucket points; the benchmark harness's
  10,000-point catch callback cannot serve as the solo-game callback.
- Any incomplete candidate invalidates a decision; report limits explicitly
  and never count truncated games as completed.
- Candidate simulation dominates the baseline; retain the current architecture
  until there is a measured reason to change it.
