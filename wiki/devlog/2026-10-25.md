  throughput trials and preserved the historical verification/output boundary.

  snapshots, source/input stability checks and archive checksum verification.

### Decisions & Learnings

  post-campaign, sampled campaign maximum and lifetime maximum RSS separately.
- Keep exact campaign allocation/GC totals separate from sampled allocation
  attribution. A zero-allocation shot probe is not a campaign allocation proof.
- Compare phase clocks in alternating pairs with identical games and inputs;
  leave decision latency and match setup clocks enabled in both variants.
