- Upstream extraction moved to `../level_segment/levels/ribbon_waves/`;

## Decisions & Learnings
- Lua static ribbon pieces receive ulp-scale phantom velocities from
  `owner + (part - owner)`; the port reproduces them (3–20 pieces per board).
- Rewriting the union-typed `Peg.shape` per step boxed the sector (~3.5 KB
  per step); pieces keep their sector in an inline `piece_shape` field.
- The level_segment generators reorder pegs so the `level_seed` shuffle
  reproduces screenshot colors. At the user's direction, Julia campaigns
  draw per-match color seeds; the fixtures keep `level_seed` for Lua parity.

# Dev Log - 2026-11-11 (2): 10ee3a0 ramp bucket, catch hold, one-ball multiball

## Progress Summary
- Ported peggle_evolved `10ee3a0` to Julia: the reshaped bucket (flat rims,
  outer ramps to the lower border, swept collision against the moving
  bucket), the caught ball falling out of view before removal, the
  `bucket_catch_hold` shot-end hold, and the one-ball green-peg multiball.
  See [bucket ramps](../context/bucket-ramps.md).
- The config artifact selects the rules. Pinned and older dev-tree configs
  keep the classic bucket and two-ball multiball, bit for bit.

## Changes Made
- `config.jl`: `RuleSet` (`RULES_CLASSIC`/`RULES_RAMP_BUCKET`), resolved once
  in `load_config` from the bucket keys present. New fields
  `bucket_rim_width`, `bucket_rim_height`, `bucket_ramp_slope_deg`,
  `bucket_catch_hold` and `bucket_catch_hold_steps`. `bucket_edge` is now
  classic-only, and `bucket_rim_color` is accepted as a presentation key.
- `state.jl`/`bucket.jl`: isbits `BucketPiece`/`BucketEdge`, plus the
  `BucketState` ramp fields and preallocated pieces. `bucket_build_pieces!`
  and `ramp_bucket_resolve!` (sweep, penetration, separate, catch zone) mirror
  Lua's operation order. `BallState.bucket_caught` is new.
- `engine.jl`: `catch_hold_until_step`, phases 11/12/15 per core.lua, and a
  bucket-model/config-rules consistency check. `powers.jl`:
  `powers_activate!` appends into the reused spawn vector.
- `checkpoint.jl`/`snapshot.jl`: per-model bucket keys (classic output is
  unchanged), `bucket_caught`, `catch_hold_until_step`. The checkpoint schema
  documents the optional keys.
- `campaigns/ribbon` re-synced to `10ee3a0` (`sync_campaign_config.jl`).
- Tests: ramp-bucket geometry and collision (mirrors `tests/test_bucket.lua`),
  config selection, one-ball powers, and engine cases (one-ball multiball,
  catch hold, real b2 catch) using Lua-captured step counts.

## Decisions & Learnings
- The rules come from config key presence, not from a version field: the
  10ee3a0 config removes `bucket_edge` and adds four simulation keys. Mixed
  or partial key sets are rejected instead of copying Lua's `or 20`/`or 0`
  defaults.
- The bucket model also travels in checkpoints (key presence, like Lua
  `copy_bucket`). An engine refuses a checkpoint whose bucket model disagrees
  with `config.rules`.
- The hold is shot-engine runtime state, not checkpoint state: `resetTurn`
  clears it, and checkpoints sit at ready-to-aim.
- `fixtures/ribbon_waves` and `fixtures/imported_levels` (captured at
  `bbd8622`) are now stale relative to the dev tree. They still replay as
  classic-rule corpora and were not rewritten.

## Verification

  steps and 20,000 swept resolves, all bit-identical.
- 25 imported-level shots captured from the dev tree at `10ee3a0` (scratch,
  with snapshots, no tooling change): Julia traces 25/25 byte-identical

- `make julia-test` 44,317/44,317; `make julia-scripts-test` 219/219;
