## Decisions & Learnings
- Lua static ribbon pieces receive ulp-scale phantom velocities from
  `owner + (part - owner)`; the port reproduces them (3–20 pieces per board).
- Rewriting the union-typed `Peg.shape` per step boxed the sector (~3.5 KB
  per step); pieces keep their sector in an inline `piece_shape` field.
- The level_segment generators reorder pegs so the `level_seed` shuffle
  reproduces screenshot colors. At the user's direction, Julia campaigns
  draw per-match color seeds; the fixtures keep `level_seed` for Lua parity.
