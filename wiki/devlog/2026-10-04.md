## Decisions & Learnings
- **Tier A math now.** `DetMath` defaults to `:system_libm` on Linux (the same
  C library LuaJIT calls); the census shows 0 mismatches over 15,794 cases.
  Julia's bundled openlibm diverges on 705 cases (4.46%), most on `atan2`
  (14.57%), so the shared-report Tier A recommendation is load-bearing, not
  cosmetic. Tier B (bundling openlibm in both engines) stays a documented
  upgrade path in `wiki/decisions/0001-math-backend.md`.
- **The RNG port is exact.** `love.math.newRandomGenerator` is Wang-hash
  seeded xorshift64* with LÖVE's `floor(r * n) + 1` integer mapping; the
  fixture pins states and bit patterns, including the purple-queue rebuild
  draws.
- **Checkpoint bytes are the geometry test.** Julia rebuilds every checkpoint
  independently from `interchange/` and matches the captured Lua records
  byte-for-byte, including `rng.state_b64`, the purple queue/active, bucket
  and fever state, trackers, match state, and ball-id allocation.
- **Reset is copy-based and alias-free.** `restore!`/`reset_worker!` copy
  every checkpoint-owned array; mutating a worker or a captured checkpoint
  cannot affect the other. `capture -> test_advance! -> restore -> capture` is
  byte-identical and repeatable for all 9 configurations.
- **Checkpoint memory is small.** `Base.summarysize` is 3.1–41.0 KiB per
  checkpoint and 132,384 B fixed scratch per worker; reset p50 is 5.7–13.9 µs
  across configurations, versus the M0 per-candidate `setupLevel` cost of
  ~82 ms on the dense annular level (~5,900×).
- **Cross-machine claim is deferred, not assumed.** No second host was
  reachable from this session (`deathstar` DNS failed). The parity manifest
  and first-difference comparator are complete and exercised on two
  independent local manifests, but the audit explicitly states that a second
  physical machine run is outstanding.
